Case Study · Backlog Clean-up

12 Months of Unreconciled Books, Cleaned Up in 3 Months

How I approach a backlog, shown as an anonymized example. Done while handling other client accounts; no client details shared.

  1. 1. Assess

    Get access to bank and card accounts, review the chart of accounts, and map which months are unreconciled.

  2. 2. Set up

    Fix the chart of accounts, check bank feeds, and set up categorization rules.

  3. 3. Categorize

    Go through transactions month by month, flagging unclear items for the client.

  4. 4. Reconcile

    Match each account to its statement, oldest month first, until balances agree.

  5. 5. Review

    Check the P&L, balance sheet and cash flow for anything that looks off, then fix it.

  6. 6. Hand off

    Deliver reports and move to regular monthly upkeep so the books don't fall behind again.
